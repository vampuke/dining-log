**R1 · MAJOR · FAIL — Update dialog reserves almost half its height as empty space.**

[UpdateDialog.kt:23](../../android/feature/src/main/kotlin/top/alanvampuke/dininglog/feature/settings/UpdateDialog.kt#L23) forces the surface to 90% of the available height. Its scrolling column at [UpdateDialog.kt:24](../../android/feature/src/main/kotlin/top/alanvampuke/dininglog/feature/settings/UpdateDialog.kt#L24) contains only a short note, progress and buttons in the supplied scenario. In [light-update.png](../bite/p8-evidence/light-update.png) and [dark-update.png](../bite/p8-evidence/dark-update.png), the last button ends around y=340 while the surface ends around y=569: roughly 230 pixels of unused surface below the controls. This confirms the defect named in the brief in both themes.

**Minimal fix:** at [UpdateDialog.kt:23](../../android/feature/src/main/kotlin/top/alanvampuke/dininglog/feature/settings/UpdateDialog.kt#L23), replace the fixed `fillMaxHeight(0.9f)` with `wrapContentHeight()` and a `heightIn(max = ...)` cap derived from the available window height. Retain bounded internal scrolling at line 24 for long notes and large fonts. Check a short idle/download state and a long-note state; short content should end shortly after the final control.

**R2 · MAJOR · FAIL — Rotation discards the editor's retained in-memory input.**

[ProductRoot.kt:165](../../android/feature/src/main/kotlin/top/alanvampuke/dininglog/feature/ProductRoot.kt#L165) calls `openEditor` from a composition effect. Activity recreation restarts that effect. [ProductViewModel.kt:170](../../android/feature/src/main/kotlin/top/alanvampuke/dininglog/feature/ProductViewModel.kt#L170) unconditionally replaces the current state with `EditorUiState(loading = true)` at line 172 and reconstructs input from defaults or cached server fields at lines 175–185. It does not recognize an already-open editor. The ViewModel is obtained with the account/generation key at [MainActivity.kt:117](../../android/app/src/main/kotlin/top/alanvampuke/dininglog/MainActivity.kt#L117), and ordinary cached-session restoration does not reset the account at [Authentication.kt:52](../../android/core/src/main/kotlin/top/alanvampuke/dininglog/core/auth/Authentication.kt#L52).

A concrete trigger is typing a restaurant name or an unsaved note, then rotating the activity. The same route opens again and replaces those fields. A failed-save message/conflict is also lost, and reopening during an in-flight save resets the visible `saving` flag. This is a static lifecycle trace, not a device reproduction. P8 permits loss after process death; removing persistence does not require losing a retained ViewModel's input on rotation.

**Minimal fix:** make [ProductViewModel.kt:170](../../android/feature/src/main/kotlin/top/alanvampuke/dininglog/feature/ProductViewModel.kt#L170) idempotent for the active `(ref, create)` editor. Track that key before launching the load and preserve input, saving/error/conflict state when it matches. Clear the key on deliberate `leaveEditor` at [ProductViewModel.kt:211](../../android/feature/src/main/kotlin/top/alanvampuke/dininglog/feature/ProductViewModel.kt#L211) and successful completion at line 232. Keep this entirely in memory. Verify repeated opening of the same route after typing, after failure and during a blocked submission.

Retain the measured inner capsule height and current bottom-inset calculation. Check both landscape directions, including a side system bar/cutout, and assert the capsule and its controls remain inside the safe horizontal bounds.
